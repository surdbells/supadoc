# VideoMed Practitioner Terms

Version 1.0

These terms apply to every doctor who consults patients through VideoMed ("the platform"). They sit alongside your professional obligations under Nigerian law and the Code of Medical Ethics issued by the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN); where the two differ, the stricter standard applies. You must accept them before your profile can be approved, and accept any new version before you take on new clinical work.

## 1. Registration and licensure

1. You must hold full registration with the MDCN and a current Annual Practising Licence (APL) for the whole time you consult on VideoMed.
2. You must upload your MDCN full-registration certificate and your current APL, and any specialist certificate you rely on for the specialty shown on your profile. Our reviewers verify them against the MDCN register before you are listed.
3. It is your duty to keep your licence current. Upload your renewed APL before the old one expires. If your licence expires, your account is suspended automatically until a current licence has been verified.
4. Tell VideoMed within 48 hours if your registration or licence is suspended, restricted, made subject to conditions, or under investigation, or if you are charged with an offence relevant to your practice.
5. The details on your profile (name, specialty, MDCN number, qualifications and certifications) must be true and complete. Once you are approved, changes to them are reviewed before they take effect.

## 2. Standard of care in telemedicine

1. You owe each patient the same standard of care as in an in-person consultation, adapted to the limits of a remote one.
2. Consult only within your specialty, training and competence, and only when you are fit to practise.
3. At the start of each consultation, confirm who the patient is and, for a minor or a patient who lacks capacity, who is consenting on their behalf.
4. Make sure the patient understands that the consultation is remote and what that means: no physical examination, the possibility of a lost connection, and when they will need to be seen in person.
5. Decide in each case whether a remote consultation is appropriate. When it is not, say so clearly and refer the patient to in-person care, a hospital or a specialist.
6. In an emergency, direct the patient to the nearest emergency department or emergency service at once, and record that you did so.
7. Consult from a private place where you cannot be overheard, with no one present whom the patient has not agreed to.

## 3. Prescribing

1. Prescribe only after an assessment adequate to support the prescription, and only within Nigerian law and the guidance of the MDCN and the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria.
2. Issue prescriptions through the platform's e-prescribing tool only, under your own name, MDCN number and signature.
3. Do not prescribe controlled drugs through VideoMed, including narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and any medicine controlled under the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency Act or listed by NAFDAC as controlled. A patient who needs one must be seen in person.
4. Prescribe antimicrobials responsibly, and only where clinically indicated.
5. Do not prescribe for yourself, your family or anyone with whom you have a personal relationship.
6. Cancel a prescription through the platform as soon as you find it is wrong, and tell the patient.

## 4. Confidentiality and data protection

1. Patient information is sensitive personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Use it only to provide the consultation and the care that follows from it.
2. Keep everything a patient tells you confidential, as required by the Code of Medical Ethics. Share it only with the patient's consent, or where the law requires or allows it.
3. Do not record, photograph, screenshot or copy a consultation or any part of a patient's record, except through the platform's own recording, which needs the consent of everyone in the call.
4. Do not download or keep patient information outside the platform, and do not use personal messaging apps or email to discuss a patient's care.
5. Keep your sign-in details secret, use a device that is locked when you step away, and sign out on shared devices.
6. Tell VideoMed immediately, and in any case within 24 hours, if you suspect that patient information has been lost, disclosed or accessed without authority, so that VideoMed can meet its own notification duties under the NDPA.

## 5. Clinical records

1. Write a complete, accurate and timely clinical note for every consultation in the platform.
2. Do not change a finalised note. Add a dated addendum instead.
3. VideoMed keeps clinical records for as long as the law requires. You may not remove records from the platform.

## 6. Professional conduct

1. Treat every patient, guest and colleague with courtesy and respect, without discrimination on any ground.
2. Do not offer or accept gifts, payments or other inducements connected with a referral, a prescription or a patient's choice of doctor.
3. Declare any conflict of interest that could affect your advice.
4. Do not ask patients to pay you outside the platform for a consultation booked on VideoMed.
5. Cooperate fully and promptly with any VideoMed review of a complaint, incident or concern about your practice.

## 7. How VideoMed processes your data

1. VideoMed processes your personal data, including your identity, contact details, registration and licence documents, payout details and a record of your actions on the platform, to verify you, list you, pay you, keep patients safe and meet its legal duties.
2. Your licensing documents are stored encrypted, are seen only by you and by the VideoMed staff who verify them, and every staff view is recorded.
3. You may ask to access or correct your personal data, or object to its processing, as the NDPA allows. Some data must be kept for as long as the law requires, even after you stop consulting on VideoMed.

## 8. Suspension

1. VideoMed may suspend your account if your licence expires or is suspended, if a complaint or concern about your conduct or competence needs review, if you breach these terms, or for another serious administrative reason. You will be told why.
2. While you are suspended you are not listed, cannot be booked and cannot do clinical work. Your future bookings are cancelled and the patients refunded and told that you are unavailable. You can still sign in to see the reason, your earnings and your payout history.
3. Where the law or our professional obligations require it, VideoMed may report a concern about your practice to the MDCN or another authority.
4. A suspension is lifted only after review, and only once a current practising licence has been verified. Cancelled bookings are not restored.

## 9. Changes to these terms

VideoMed may update these terms. Each update has a new version number. You will be asked to read and accept the new version before you take on new clinical work.

## 10. Acceptance

By accepting these terms you confirm that you hold full MDCN registration and a current Annual Practising Licence, that the information on your profile is true, and that you will practise on VideoMed in line with these terms.
